## What to build
Hand-back, outside the budget:
- `wrapup` runs at every kind of run end.
- The round analyst, in distill mode, writes `DISTILL_SPEC.md`: the retained mechanisms and their tuned values, how they fit the project's idioms, what to drop, and a keep-or-drop decision for each non-lever change. It's recorded as `distill_spec`, then reviewed against the brief.
- The lever coder, in distill mode, implements it on `bo-research/<run-id>-distilled`, taken from the base commit, with no harness dependency and protected paths untouched.
- Verification: 3 replicates of each branch at the reference fidelity, agreeing within 2σ. Up to 2 retries, otherwise the branch is marked unverified and the research branch is the confirmed result.
- `REPORT.md` in 8 sections, including the price of a discouraged winner taken from logged trials.
- `--no-confirm` marks the result unconfirmed. `clean` removes the worktree and venv, and keeps the branches and log.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Planting a distillation bug triggers retries, then `unverified`, with the research branch as the confirmed result.
- [ ] `REPORT.md` sections appear in order, and the headline cites the confirming replicates.
- [ ] Wrap-up trials are not charged to the budget.
- [ ] `clean` keeps the branches and `log.db`.
