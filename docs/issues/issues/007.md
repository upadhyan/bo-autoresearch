## Question

How does the loop make sure a reject verdict isn't one unlucky trial?

Decide seed handling, when and how often the **harness** (not an agent) schedules replicate trials, how run-to-run noise is estimated (for example in the pre-loop baseline), and how noise is fed to the sampler (Optuna has no native noisy objective). 2608.00316 has no replication at all; its only noise handling is the GP noise estimate. So this can't be left to agent judgment.

## Resolution

**Gist:** Noise handling belongs to the harness, not to agents. Every trial gets a fresh logged seed. Noise is estimated before the loop from baseline replicates, re-estimated every round, and it anchors a noise-aware verdict GP. Replicates are a harness-scheduled floor, plus confirmation of every new incumbent. The agent's job is to propose the **minimum meaningful experiment**, a fidelity ladder. The harness validates the ladder against the reference fidelity and picks the rung with the lowest expected cost to a verdict.

### Seeds and noise estimation

1. **Seeds:** the harness draws a fresh seed per trial from a study-level RNG, logs it, and passes it to the runner. The seed is never a lever, and the runner has to accept one.
2. **Baseline noise:** before the loop, the baseline config runs k=5 times per candidate fidelity rung with different seeds, and the user can change k in pre-loop grilling. If the user declares the objective deterministic, or all k runs agree within float tolerance, σ≈0 and replication is turned off.
3. **Re-estimation:** σ is re-estimated every round from that round's replicates. The baseline σ seeds round 1 and serves as a sanity floor. A shift of more than 2× against the previous round is logged and flagged in the round summary.

### Replicates

4. **Scheduling:** the harness picks replicates from the top-ranked evaluated configs. They take at least ~10% of the round budget, and more when the fidelity trade-off (item 9) calls for it. Every new incumbent gets r=2 confirmation replicates before it counts as the best. Agents never request replicates.
5. **Storage:** a **replicate** is its own trial, linked to the original by `replicate_of`, and is never averaged in.

### Noise model

6. **Feeding noise to the models:**
   - Search uses Optuna `GPSampler(deterministic_objective=False)`, which learns homoscedastic noise.
   - The verdict GP, which runs off-Optuna for Sobol, uses the baseline σ² as a lower bound or prior on its noise term.
7. **Heteroscedastic noise:** v1 assumes constant noise. If replicate spread differs by more than ~3× across regions, the surrogate-fit gate fails and the verdict is **inconclusive**, never reject. Modelling noise as a function of the levers is out of scope for v1.

### Fidelity (minimum meaningful experiment)

8. **Ladder and validation:** the agent proposes a fidelity ladder (e.g. 15 min / 1 h / 5 h), using domain judgement about which rungs can resolve the effects in question. The harness then runs a calibration of m≈5 diverse configs at each candidate rung and at the reference fidelity. A rung passes only if both checks hold:
   - **Rank fidelity:** Spearman ρ ≥ 0.8 against the reference fidelity.
   - **Resolvability:** the spread across configs is at least 3σ at that rung.

   If no rung passes, the loop falls back to the reference fidelity and flags it. Skipping calibration requires the user to state explicitly that they accept an unvalidated proxy, and that acceptance is logged.
9. **Rung choice:** among passing rungs, the harness picks the one that minimises expected cost to a verdict: cost per trial × trials needed, where the replicates needed scale with σ²/effect². This is how "1×15 min beats 3×5 h" gets decided.
10. **Fidelity-sensitive hypotheses:** at registration the agent may declare a hypothesis **fidelity-sensitive**, with a reason, when its mechanism may only pay off late (schedules, regularisation). Such a hypothesis can never be rejected at a proxy fidelity. It can only be inconclusive there, and it is retested at a higher rung.
11. **Proxy drift check:** every round re-runs the incumbent and one random config at the reference fidelity. If rank agreement degrades, the proxy is flagged as broken and that round's rejects are downgraded to inconclusive.
12. **Fidelity changes:** fidelity may change only at a round boundary, and a fidelity change ends the round. Trials from the old rung stay in the log as telemetry and are **not** warm-started. Multi-fidelity BO, with fidelity as a sampled dimension, is out of scope for v1.
13. **Early stopping:** performance-based pruning is off, because it biases the verdict GP toward whatever looks good early. Only degenerate trials (NaN, divergence) stop early, and they are marked failed, not pruned.

### Knock-on for open tickets

- **Warm-start policy:** only trials at the round's fidelity are eligible for warm start.
- **Experiment log schema:** needs per-trial `seed`, `replicate_of`, `fidelity`, and per-round σ and calibration records.
- **Pre-loop grilling script:** needs to cover the fidelity ladder, the calibration cost, k, and the deterministic-objective declaration.
- **Agent roster and contracts:** someone has to propose the ladder and declare fidelity sensitivity at registration.
- **Round termination rule:** is now unblocked. The incumbent confirmation replicates and the reference-fidelity drift check both land at the end of a round.
