## What to build
The calibration round (R0) and the budget ledger:
- `smoke` runs one trial at baseline.
- k baseline replicates (default 5) give σ. Declaring the objective deterministic, or getting agreeing replicates, sets σ≈0 and turns replication off.
- Fidelity calibration: about 5 diverse configs at each rung and at the reference fidelity. A rung passes on Spearman ρ ≥ 0.8 and spread ≥ 3σ, and the passing rung with the lowest expected cost to a verdict is chosen. If none passes, fall back to the reference and flag it. Accepting an unvalidated proxy requires an explicit logged flag.
- The budget ledger is computed from the log (summed trial wall-clock time). A trial is refused when its estimated cost, from the calibrated rung, exceeds the remaining budget.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] On a toy with known noise, R0 logs `noise_estimate` within tolerance of the planted σ.
- [ ] A toy whose cheap rung has scrambled ranks fails calibration. A faithful cheap rung is chosen when it's cheaper to a verdict.
- [ ] With no rung passing, the loop falls back to the reference fidelity and flags it. `proxy_accepted_unvalidated` is logged only when requested.
- [ ] A trial whose estimated cost exceeds the remaining budget is refused, and the refusal is logged.
- [ ] A deterministic declaration turns off replicates.
