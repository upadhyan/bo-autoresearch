## What to build
`round-run`, where BO does the searching:
- The round's Optuna study is rebuilt from the log (disposable storage), seeded with `create_trial`/`add_trials`, with each seed bounds-checked by the harness.
- One eligibility function (same epoch, same fidelity, in range) feeds the sampler.
- `GPSampler` with a non-deterministic objective.
- Replicate scheduling: at least ~10% of trials go to replicates, and every new incumbent gets 2 confirmation replicates. Replicates carry `replicate_of` and are never averaged in.
- A drift check each round: re-run the incumbent and one random config at the reference fidelity, and flag the proxy as broken if rank agreement degrades.
- End triggers: a stall (no gain ≥ δ over max(5·d_total, 20) fresh trials), and a cap of ~25% of the remaining budget.
- `round-run` returns JSON with the trigger, the incumbent and the `next` list.
- Crash resume: heartbeats, `trial_abandoned` on resume (charged up to the last heartbeat and never retried), and state rebuilt from the log.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] On a toy with one active hypothesis, `round-run` improves on random search at equal trial count, averaged over seeds.
- [ ] The study is fully reconstructable from the log. Deleting the study file and resuming gives identical eligible trials.
- [ ] Replicates make up at least ~10% of trials, and each new incumbent has 2 confirmation replicates.
- [ ] Killing the process mid-trial and resuming logs `trial_abandoned` and charges the budget.
- [ ] A stall ends the round, never the run.
- [ ] The round cap ends a round at about 25% of the remaining budget.
