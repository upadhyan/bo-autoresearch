## What to build
The loop's guard against myopia:
- Every reject, inconclusive or park emits a removal event.
- `round-run` is refused while any removal lacks a recorded interplay review.
- Each flagged pair links the removed hypothesis to a partner. When the partner activates, the removed hypothesis is revived as a new linked version into the same round, at most once per pair.
- Flagging revivals at registration: past removals flagged against a newcomer are revived the same way.
- Hypotheses flagged as interacting are placed in the same round.
- Generation triggers appear in `next`: run start, the queue below 2× the slots, or an analyst flag.
- `generation: scripted` serves fixture proposals in fixed passes.
- The exhaustion rule: the queue is empty, no active hypothesis is undecided, every removal has its review, every revival has run, and the final generation pass produced nothing that survived review.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Toy: A is rejected alone. When B registers and the review flags A, A is revived with B, and both are retained.
- [ ] Toy: C is removed while D is queued. The interplay review flags D, and C is revived into D's round.
- [ ] A pair is revived at most once.
- [ ] `round-run` is refused while a removal lacks its review.
- [ ] Exhaustion never fires while any condition fails, and fires once all of them hold. A stall never ends the run.
- [ ] Scripted generation serves passes in order.
