## What to build
Limits on what the research may try:
- A versioned directive registry holding directives (severity, statement, reason, scope, optional predicate, optional forbidden patterns), protected paths and the research brief. It's written at `init` and protected.
- `register` is refused without a `review` record (directive verdict, intent verdict, conflict class).
  - An off-intent verdict parks the hypothesis. A prune verdict prunes it.
  - A stretch or deprioritize verdict lowers priority and requires a stated reason.
- Prohibited limits:
  - lever ranges and options must lie inside the allowed region, or registration is refused;
  - seeds outside the region are dropped;
  - every trial's resolved config is checked against the prohibited predicates;
  - commit diffs are scanned for forbidden patterns.
- Discouraged limits: a per-trial `compat:<id>` flag computed from the resolved config.
- Registry revisions take effect at round boundaries: prune, park, make trials ineligible, re-review, then end the round.
- The protected-path hash manifest, recorded at `init` and re-checked before every round and every commit.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Registration without a review is refused.
- [ ] A pruned hypothesis never produces a trial.
- [ ] A lever range crossing a prohibited predicate is refused. A trial whose config violates a predicate is refused before it runs.
- [ ] A commit containing a forbidden pattern is refused.
- [ ] `compat` flags appear on every trial, and are recomputed after a revision.
- [ ] Tampering with a protected file (by any means) is caught by the manifest at the next round or commit.
- [ ] An off-intent verdict parks the hypothesis, and taste never prunes.
