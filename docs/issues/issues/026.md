## What to build
The fixed reject form:
- A noise-aware verdict GP, outside Optuna, with the baseline σ² as a floor on its noise term.
- √V_T: the unnormalised group total-order Sobol variance.
- Δ: the best posterior objective with the group free, minus the best with the group at baseline, with the other active levers optimised in both.
- Gates: trial count (sampler and agent-chosen), range coverage (sampler trials only), surrogate fit, and noise homogeneity.
- Burn-in: max(10·d, 20) fresh sampler trials. A reject is confirmed after max(5·d, 10) more. Verdict checks then happen every max(5·d, 10).
- Reject when the 95% upper bound of √V_T is below δ (`irrelevant`) or the 95% upper bound of Δ is below δ (`no-improvement`, which freezes the group at baseline). Retain when both lower bounds are above δ. Otherwise the hypothesis stays active.
- Freezing a single lever whose own upper bound is below δ.
- Verdict records `V-R<n>-H<n>.v<k>-<check>`, with every field in the spec.
- The `retained-against-prediction` flag. No rejects at a proxy for fidelity-sensitive hypotheses. A broken proxy downgrades rejects to inconclusive.
- Escalation: a larger replicate share, then a higher rung, then inconclusive at max(40·d, 80).
- A search-space change ends the round, and a pending reject holds it open.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Planted toys, over fixed seeds: a useful lever is retained; a useless lever is rejected `irrelevant`; a harmful one is rejected `no-improvement`; lever A, which helps only with a co-active B, is never rejected while both are active; a fidelity-sensitive lever is never rejected at a proxy.
- [ ] No verdict is recorded before burn-in, and every reject has a confirming check logged.
- [ ] A contradicted prediction flags a retain without changing it.
- [ ] Noise that differs by region fails the homogeneity gate, giving inconclusive rather than reject.
- [ ] A stuck hypothesis escalates and becomes inconclusive at the cap.
- [ ] Verdict records contain every field in the spec.
