# Issue #8: Reject-condition form

- State: CLOSED
- Author: upadhyan
- Created: 2026-09-25T02:56:56Z

## Question

What form does a **reject condition** take, and who evaluates it?

Options include free text judged by a subagent, a machine-checkable rule evaluated by the harness (e.g. lever importance < ε over ≥N trials, flat partial dependence), or a hybrid. It must account for interactions: "lever A only matters when B is on" must not reject A. Decide also how the author picks thresholds before seeing data, and how verdicts cite evidence. Judges must cite the condition against telemetry, so they can't rubber-stamp. 2608.00316 has no judges, but its agent lazily deferred to the surrogate unless prompted otherwise.

## Resolution

**Gist:** a reject condition is not something the author writes. The harness applies one fixed pair of statistics to every hypothesis, measured against a single **minimum meaningful effect δ** that the user sets:
- **√V_T** checks whether the hypothesis's levers matter.
- **Δ** checks whether they help.

The author pre-registers the levers, ranges, baseline, mechanism, a **predicted direction** for each graded lever, and the fidelity-sensitive flag. Verdicts wait for a burn-in of fresh sampler trials, and a reject must be confirmed by a second check. Judges interpret a harness **verdict record** and must quote it exactly.

### Statistics and threshold
1. **Absolute scale.** The irrelevance statistic is √V_T: the square root of the *unnormalised* group total-order Sobol variance from the noise-aware verdict GP, in objective units. The normalised index is telemetry only, because a dominant lever elsewhere would otherwise shrink real effects below a relative τ.
   - This **replaces τ** in the [Hypothesis lifecycle](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/6) and [interaction-detection research](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/3). Retain, reject and lever freezing all compare against δ.
2. **δ is set once by the user**, in pre-loop grilling.
   - The script suggests a starting value from the baseline σ, e.g. 2σ.
   - δ is global. Agents can't set it or override it per hypothesis.
   - Before the loop, the harness warns if δ is so small next to σ that a verdict would need an impractical number of trials, since cost scales with σ²/δ².
3. **Improvement contrast Δ.** Δ = (best posterior objective with the group free) − (best with the group at baseline), with the other active levers optimised in both cases. It is estimated from GP posterior samples over a quasi-random candidate set. Because the other levers are optimised in both terms, "A only helps when B is on" still gives Δ_A > 0.

### Conditions
4. **Reject** if, at 95% one-sided credibility, the gates pass and either condition holds:
   - `irrelevant`: the upper bound of √V_T is below δ.
   - `no-improvement`: the upper bound of Δ is below δ. The group's levers are then frozen at baseline.
5. **Retain** only if the gates pass and the lower bounds of both √V_T and Δ are above δ.
6. **Otherwise the hypothesis stays active.** At the end of research it becomes inconclusive, as already decided.
7. **Fidelity-sensitive hypotheses** can't be rejected at a proxy fidelity, per [Noise and replication policy](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/7).

### What the author pre-registers
8. At registration the author supplies:
   - the levers, with their ranges;
   - the baseline;
   - the `mechanism`;
   - the fidelity-sensitive flag, with a reason;
   - a **predicted direction** for every graded lever. Categorical levers are exempt.

   There are no free-text conditions and no menu of extra machine conditions in v1.
9. **A contradicted prediction flags the verdict but doesn't change it.** The verdict stays retained, flagged `retained-against-prediction`. The round summary then prompts the orchestrator to register a rival explanation.

### Repeated checking
10. **Burn-in.** No verdict is computed until the hypothesis has at least max(10·d, 20) fresh sampler trials at the current fidelity since it became active. Here d is the number of levers in the group. Warm-started and agent-chosen trials feed the GP but don't count toward the burn-in. Until then the verdict record says `burn-in`.
11. **Confirming check.** A reject must hold again at a second check, after at least max(5·d, 10) more fresh sampler trials. Retains need no confirming check, because they are re-judged every round anyway.
12. How often the harness checks within a round is left to **Round termination rule**.

### Evidence citation
13. **Verdict record.** Each round the harness writes one per hypothesis, containing:
    - the record id;
    - the condition that fired, if any;
    - √V_T and Δ, each with its credible interval;
    - δ;
    - the status of each gate: trial count (split into sampler and agent-chosen), range coverage, surrogate fit and noise homogeneity;
    - burn-in and confirmation status;
    - the fidelity, and whether it is a proxy;
    - the prediction flag;
    - the trial ids used.
14. **Judges** write a schema-checked note that references the verdict record's id and quotes its numbers. A `SubagentStop` hook exits with code 2 if any quoted number doesn't match the record. Judges add interpretation only, such as why a lever is flat or which rival to register next.

### Glossary
Added **Minimum meaningful effect**, **Predicted direction** and **Verdict record**. Tightened **Retained**: the levers must both matter and improve the objective beyond δ.

### Knock-on for open tickets
- **Experiment log schema:** add verdict records, δ, per-lever predicted direction, burn-in and confirmation counters, and fresh-sampler trial counts.
- **Pre-loop grilling script:** have the user set δ, starting from the σ-based suggestion, and warn about infeasible δ.
- **Agent roster and contracts:** add the judge output schema and the `SubagentStop` quote check. The author contract requires a predicted direction for each graded lever.
- **Round termination rule:** set how often verdicts are checked within a round. A pending reject confirmation is a candidate reason to keep a round open.
- **Runner harness API:** compute √V_T and Δ, and emit verdict records.
- **Final deliverable:** report `retained-against-prediction` flags and `no-improvement` rejects separately from `irrelevant` ones.

**Amended by** [the irrelevance-statistic amendment on the spec](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/21#issuecomment-5890844933): √V_T is replaced by the total-effect range M_u (the largest change in the latent posterior as the group moves over its box, over every setting of the other levers), which is on δ's scale for every effect shape. `irrelevant` becomes `no-improvement` **and** UB(M_u) < δ, retain is LB(Δ) > δ, a single lever is frozen on UB(Δ_i) < δ, and co-active `no-improvement` rejects apply one per round end. √V_T stays as telemetry.
