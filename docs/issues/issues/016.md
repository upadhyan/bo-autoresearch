**upadhyan** (2026-09-28T22:35:35Z):

## Resolution

**Gist:** a finished run hands back three things:
- a **distilled branch**: a clean re-implementation of what worked, written from a reviewed spec, with no research scaffolding and no `boautoresearch` dependency. It is delivered only after the harness has reproduced the research result on it.
- the configurable **research branch**.
- **`REPORT.md`**, **`trials.csv`** and **`hypotheses.csv`**.

**Wrap-up is outside the budget.** The budget covers only the research loop, so the ~5% reserve is gone.

### Budget
1. **The budget covers only the research loop**, from the calibration round to the end of the run. Wrap-up is unbudgeted and uncapped: distillation, verification, the final confirmation and report generation.
   - The ~5% reserve is **removed**. The harness refuses a trial only when its estimated cost exceeds what's left of the budget.
   - This amends [Round termination rule](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/10) (the reserve, and run-end condition i) and [Pre-loop grilling script](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/15) (no reserve to confirm).

### Distillation (replaces the revert-based distilled branch of [Code-change management](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/12))
The distilled branch contains only the **retained** hypotheses, integrated cleanly into the codebase. Each step below is required by the harness's `next` list, and no new roles are added.

2. **Spec.** The round analyst, in distill mode, writes `DISTILL_SPEC.md` from the verdict records and the research branch's diff. It covers:
   - each retained mechanism and its tuned values;
   - how it fits into the project's own idioms, such as its existing config system and the naming of its hyperparameters;
   - what to drop: levers frozen at baseline, dead code paths, the lever scaffolding;
   - a keep-or-drop decision, with a reason, for each non-lever change (`commit_change` refactors and bug fixes).

   It is recorded as `distill_spec`, with verdict ids cited and quote-checked.
3. **Review.** The registration reviewer checks the spec against the research brief (especially the complexity appetite) and the directives.
4. **Implementation.** The lever coder, in distill mode, implements the spec on a fresh `bo-research/<run-id>-distilled` branch taken from the **base commit**, not by reverting commits.
   - The resulting project code has no `boautoresearch` dependency.
   - Protected paths (objective, evaluation, data) stay untouched, so the verification means something.
   - The harness supplies the runner from outside. It isn't part of the delivered branch.
5. **Verification.** The harness runs r replicates (default 3) of the distilled code at the reference fidelity, alongside r replicates of the research branch's incumbent. These double as the **final confirmation**.
   - It passes when the two means agree within 2σ, scaled for r.
   - On failure, the coder gets the gap and the diff, and has up to **2** more attempts.
   - If it still fails, the distilled branch is delivered marked **unverified**, the research branch at the incumbent's values is the confirmed result, and the report explains the gap.
6. **Mechanical inlining is dropped**, and so is committing the incumbent's values to `levers.json` on the distilled branch (amends [Runner harness API](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/13), item 2). The research branch keeps `lever()` and `levers.json` at baseline, so anyone who wants to keep tuning still can.
7. **Nothing is merged into the user's branches.**

### Discouraged directives (amends [Directives: prohibited vs discouraged vs preferred](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/18), item 8)
8. **If the winner violates a discouraged directive**, the report flags it and gives the best **already-logged** trial that honours the directive, with the gap and its interval, all from the log.
   - There are no extra confirmation runs and no alternative is delivered.
   - The report offers a one-line "distill this alternative" follow-up.
   - The point is to show the price of honouring the directive, without shipping a second answer.

### `trials.csv`
9. **One row per trial of every kind**: calibration, baseline, seed, sampler, agent-chosen, replicate, smoke and confirmation, including failed and abandoned trials.
10. **Columns:**
    - **Metadata:** `seq`, `round`, `epoch`, `commit`, `fidelity`, `seed`, `replicate_of`, `chosen_by`, `status`, `objective`, `wall_clock_s` and `peak_mem`.
    - **Prefixed groups:** `c:<constraint>`, `t:<telemetry>`, `compat:<directive>`, and one `L:<lever>` column per lever that ever existed, holding its **effective** value.
    - **Masked levers** also get a `sampled:<lever>` column.
11. **What a lever cell means:**
    - **Empty** means the lever didn't exist in that trial's code.
    - **The baseline value** means the code existed and ran at baseline, e.g. the lever was parked or pinned.
    - The CSV never fills in values after the fact. Warm-start backfill happens only inside the harness.

### `hypotheses.csv`
12. **One row per hypothesis version**, with:
    - id, title, lens, provenance and mechanism;
    - final state and reason, with `irrelevant` and `no-improvement` kept separate;
    - the √V_T and Δ bounds, and the trials used;
    - whether the expected verdict was right, `revived_from`, and any prediction flag.

### `REPORT.md`
13. **The harness generates the mechanical sections.** The round analyst writes a **Takeaways** section of at most 5 bullets citing verdict ids, which the quote check enforces. The sections, in order:
    1. **Headline:** the confirmed result at the reference fidelity, from the distilled branch if verified and the research branch otherwise, against the baseline, in objective units and in δ. Plus the run-end reason and the budget used.
    2. **What to take:**
       - both branch names and whether the distilled branch was verified;
       - the retained mechanisms and their tuned values;
       - the non-lever changes kept or dropped, with reasons;
       - any violations of discouraged directives, with the price of honouring each.
    3. **Takeaways.**
    4. **What worked and what didn't:** the final `SUMMARY.md` sections, expanded with:
       - rejects split into `irrelevant` and `no-improvement`;
       - hypotheses retained against their prediction;
       - hypotheses made inconclusive by the evidence cap;
       - parked hypotheses (including off-intent ones) with their reasons;
       - pruned hypotheses;
       - untested hypotheses, if the run ended early.
    5. **Interactions and revivals:** each interplay flag and what came of it.
    6. **Terms of the run:** the brief, the directives, δ, the budget, R0's results (σ and calibration), and any proposed directives.
    7. **Process diagnostics:** expected-verdict accuracy, yield per lens, failure rates, epochs and equivalence-check failures.
    8. **Files:** `DISTILL_SPEC.md`, `trials.csv`, `hypotheses.csv`, `rounds/` and `log.db`.

### Ending early and cleaning up
14. **Every run end produces the same deliverable**, including a user stop in the middle of a round.
    - `wrapup` always runs distillation and verification, unless the user passes `--no-confirm`. The report then marks the result **unconfirmed**.
15. **Everything stays in place**: the worktree, the venv and `.bo-research/`.
    - `boautoresearch clean <run>` removes the worktree and the venv.
    - The branches and the log are always kept.

### Glossary
- **Distilled branch** now reads as a clean, verified re-implementation of what worked.
- **Budget** now excludes wrap-up.

### Knock-on for open tickets
- **Enforcement hooks:**
  - During distillation, the lever coder writes on the distilled branch's own worktree. The protected-path block applies there too.
  - The worktree-writes-during-a-round block doesn't apply during wrap-up.
  - The `Stop` hook must also block until distillation and verification have finished, or `--no-confirm` was given.
