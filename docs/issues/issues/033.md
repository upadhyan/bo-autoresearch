## What to build
Human-readable outputs, regenerated from the log:
- `SUMMARY.md` after every state change. Sections: Where we are, Working, Not working (with the reason in words), Still testing, Set aside, Up next. One line per hypothesis title, at most 10 lines per section.
- Round summaries in `rounds/NNN.md`. Mechanical sections: the trigger, verdict records, incumbent, σ, budget, suggested narrowings, and expected versus actual verdicts. Plus a slot for the analyst narrative, filled from a `narrative` record.
- The full `trials.csv` schema:
  - metadata columns;
  - prefixed groups `c:`, `t:`, `compat:` and `L:` (effective values), plus `sampled:` for masked levers;
  - empty means the lever didn't exist, and baseline means it ran at baseline.
- `hypotheses.csv`, one row per version.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `SUMMARY.md` changes after every verdict, and matches the state tables.
- [ ] Section caps are respected.
- [ ] `trials.csv` includes calibration, replicate, smoke and failed rows. Lever cells follow the empty-versus-baseline rule.
- [ ] `hypotheses.csv` keeps `irrelevant` and `no-improvement` distinct.
- [ ] Deleting every generated file and rebuilding from the log reproduces them byte for byte.
