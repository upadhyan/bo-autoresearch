## Fixes found by the live runs
- Skill: the round-run wait loop never ended on Linux (procps `pgrep` matched its own shell) and could match another run's round.
- Benchmark: the planted truth moved out of the toy repo (`$DOGFOOD_SCORER`); `check.py` fails a run whose agents saw it; runs are isolated from user hooks/plugins/MCP (`--setting-sources project,local --strict-mcp-config`); `stdin=DEVNULL`.
- Generators stay in their lens's lane; subagents are spawned in the foreground (no held-Stop `hook_blocked` noise).
- A free run can end by exhaustion (empty `record proposal`); a subagent's `record` through a shell variable is refused; a hypothesis can't be parked before its rung escalation is judged.

## Known limits closed
- **Parallel trials:** `workers` > 1 runs a BO round's trials concurrently (budget = summed per-trial compute time). Sequential (`workers: 1`) stays the default and the interview recommends it.
- **#29:** retained hypotheses are narrowed when the dimension cap is tight; queued trials an interrupted round never started carry to the resuming round.
- **#28:** a new epoch re-measures σ per rung at first use and marks the ladder calibration stale; `add-dependency` updates `uv.lock` / `poetry.lock`.
- **#26 / #38:** the evidence cap carries across rung changes and freezes; freezes at a broken/unchecked proxy fidelity are undone; the fidelity-sensitive exemption is bounded (escalate a rung at the cap).
- **#34 / #37 enforcement:** the orchestrator's raw-log reads through Bash (command substitution, names, globs) and Read/Grep/Glob globs are refused; the distilled branch refuses dynamic harness imports.

## Spec amendments (posted on #21)
- [Two-stage t-test on a pooled σ, α = 5%](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/21#issuecomment-5896122041) for the equivalence check, branch verification and drift check (false epochs 15% → 2.5–3.6%); the drift pair can no longer be a tie.
- [Calibrated verdict intervals](https://github.com/upadhyan/bo-autoresearch/issues/21#issuecomment-5897420132): a δ-based signal floor on the verdict GP (a gain of δ at σ = 1.5δ was rejected 10/20, now 0/20); `no-improvement` is the expected label for a flat lever next to a curved partner.
