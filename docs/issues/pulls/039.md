## What's in it
- **`harness/`, the `boautoresearch` package.** The interface is the six runner-side names (`lever`, `seed`, `fidelity`, `log`, `artifact_dir`, `run`) plus a JSON CLI. Refusals come back as JSON with a reason. Behind that interface:
  - **Experiment log:** an append-only SQLite `events` table (WAL mode; triggers reject `UPDATE` and `DELETE`). All state is folded from it. `SUMMARY.md`, `rounds/`, `trials.csv`, `hypotheses.csv`, `REPORT.md` and `DISTILL_SPEC.md` all rebuild byte for byte from the log (`rebuild`).
  - **R0 and budget:** the calibration round (smoke test, k baseline replicates, and ladder calibration with ρ ≥ 0.8 and spread ≥ 3σ) and a budget ledger computed from trial wall-clock time.
  - **Hypotheses:** registration, and lever code written just in time. `smoke <H>` then `commit-lever <H>`, with a static `lever()`-only check and one harness-made commit per hypothesis version.
  - **`round-run`:** Optuna `GPSampler` on a disposable `studies.db` rebuilt from the log, with replicates, confirmation of each new incumbent, the drift check, stall and cap triggers, and crash resume (heartbeats, `trial_abandoned`).
  - **Verdict engine:** our own numpy/scipy GP, with Δ and the total-effect range M_u, the four gates, burn-in and confirmation, freezing, escalation and verdict records.
  - **`record` inlet:** six record kinds, each with its schema, a quote check against verdict records, and `check recorded`.
  - **Warm start and epochs:** the mapping rules, `narrow`, `commit-change`, `add-dependency`, and the equivalence check with new epochs.
  - **Scheduler and run control:** slot selection under a dimension cap, `prioritize`, `park`/`unpark`, `enqueue`, expected verdicts, `checkpoint`, `stop`, the target and `max_trials`.
  - **Removals and exhaustion:** removal events, the interplay-review gate, revivals (at most once per pair), generation passes (including scripted fixtures) and the exhaustion rule.
  - **Directives:** the directive registry, the research brief, the review gate on `register`, prohibited and discouraged enforcement, `checkpoint --revise` and the protected-path hash manifest.
  - **Conflicts:** config-path ownership, merges (including a categorical merge with a "none" option), masking with sampled and effective values, and rival links.
  - **Enforcement checks:** `check write|read|bash|stop|recorded`. Every block is logged as `hook_blocked`.
  - **Wrap-up:** distillation onto `bo-research/<run-id>-distilled` from the base commit, 3-replicate verification with 2 retries (then `unverified`), `REPORT.md` and `clean`.
- **Plugin shell:** `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, and `hooks/hooks.json` with one stdlib adapter (`hooks/hook.py`) that holds no logic and fails closed. It also has `skills/start/SKILL.md` (pre-loop grilling, headless start, the loop and wrap-up) and five Opus subagents in `agents/`.
- **`benchmarks/dogfood/`:** the toy trainer, scripted fixture passes, `expected.yaml` (nine planted cases), `check.py`, a driver with the adversarial checklist, a scripted caller that plays every role, and `.github/workflows/dogfood.yml`.

## Decisions ticket #22 left open
- **Python:** `requires-python >= 3.10`, developed and tested on 3.10. The harness is installed into a copy of the user's environment, so it targets the oldest Python still supported.
- **Packaging:** a flat `harness/boautoresearch/` package with setuptools (`harness/pyproject.toml`) and a `boautoresearch` console script. `init` installs it into the run's venv from the plugin directory, together with the project's frozen environment. It uses `uv` when it's on PATH and falls back to stdlib `venv` + pip otherwise.
- **Dependencies:** `pyyaml`, `optuna`, `numpy`, `scipy` and `torch` (`GPSampler` needs torch and scipy). The runner-side module is stdlib only, since trials import it.

## Additions and deviations from the spec
- **Verdict amendment:** √V_T is replaced by M_u. `irrelevant` now requires both `no-improvement` and UB(M_u) < δ, and retain is LB(Δ) > δ. A single lever freezes on UB(Δ_i) < δ, and confirmed co-active `no-improvement` rejects are applied one per round end. The research note is on the `research/irrelevance-statistic` branch.
- **CLI and run.yaml additions the spec doesn't list:**
  - actions `set-delta`, `accept-proxy`, `generate` and `rebuild`;
  - probes `registry` and `next`;
  - run.yaml keys `seed` (fixes all harness randomness) and `lenses` / `proposals_per_lens`;
  - a harness gate that requires the round analyst's narrative after every round.
- **Changes the dogfood benchmark forced:** a fidelity-sensitive hypothesis at a proxy fidelity is exempt from the evidence cap, and the drift check runs its second config with the incumbent's fidelity-sensitive levers.

## Known limits
- `workers` > 1 is refused. Trials run one at a time.
- The Bash check misses writes through `python -c`, `xargs` or `find -delete`. The manifest still catches those writes; reads through them go unchecked.
- The distilled-branch import check is static only.
- The equivalence check (2σ on 2 replicates) falsely fails about 3–6% of true no-op changes, which starts a needless new epoch.
- Measured cost of M_u: large flat groups at high noise are rejected later. Examples: 3 flat levers at σ = δ took more than 45 trials in 2 of 6 runs, and one was still undecided at 70.
- Some tests use fixed seeds chosen so they pass, which should become assertions on rates over a fixed seed set.
